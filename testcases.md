Validate ECMP support for IPv4 and IPv6. | 1. All routes installed in forwarding table as ECMP paths (`show ip route <prefix>`, `ip route show <prefix>`).<br>2. | 2.3.3 | ECMP Failover and Convergence | Failover and Convergence | Test ECMP failover and convergence by simulating path loss, restoration, dynamic route changes, and device reboot to ensure fast recovery and consistent forwarding. | 3 Nodes | 1.
